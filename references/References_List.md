# Key References for DNA Storage

Here are some of the most prominent references utilized in the research and construction of this term paper and presentation. 

1. **J. Sima, N. Raviv, M. Schwartz, and J. Bruck**, "Error Correction for DNA Storage," *IEEE BITS the Information Theory Magazine*, 2023. (The primary paper assigned to our group).
2. **C. E. Shannon**, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication," *The Bell System Technical Journal*, 1948. (Foundational information theory governing error channels).
3. **V. I. Levenshtein**, "Binary codes capable of correcting deletions, insertions, and reversals," *Soviet Physics Doklady*, 1966. (Pioneering work on synchronization errors).
4. **R. R. Varshamov and G. M. Tenengolts**, "Codes which correct single asymmetric errors," *Automation and Remote Control*, 1965. (The foundation of VT codes for single-deletion correction).
5. **G. M. Church, Y. Gao, and S. Kosuri**, "Next-generation digital information storage in DNA," *Science*, 2012. (One of the earliest successful implementations of in-vitro DNA storage).
6. **N. Goldman et al.**, "Towards practical, high-capacity, low-maintenance information storage in synthesized DNA," *Nature*, 2013.
7. **S. Jain, F. Farnoud, M. Schwartz, and J. Bruck**, "Duplication-correcting codes for data storage in the DNA of living organisms," *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, 2017. (Key analysis of in-vivo duplication error mechanics).
8. **J. Sima, R. Gabrys, and J. Bruck**, "Optimal systematic t-deletion correcting codes," *IEEE ISIT*, 2020. (Mathematical framing of indicator vectors and high-order sums).
9. **R. Heckel, G. Mikutis, and R. N. Grass**, "A characterization of the DNA data storage channel," *Scientific Reports*, 2019.
10. **A. Lenz, A. Wachter-Zeh, and E. Yaakobi**, "Duplication-correcting codes," *Designs, Codes and Cryptography*, 2019.
